Seeking meaningful, mission-driven work.

I am a product manager with deep experience in enterprise payment systems, financial operations, and technical product delivery — and I am actively seeking roles in government and public-sector organizations where that experience can drive meaningful, lasting impact.

I am a collaborative, reliable builder — someone who reads documentation carefully, asks the right questions early, and delivers. I'd welcome the opportunity to bring that to your team.
