San Diego Photographer

Hi, I'm
Brianna.

Born and raised in San Diego. Always at the beach. Always taking pictures of someone.

Where It Started

It started at family parties and events.

Growing up, I was always the one making my family and friends pose for portraits — at Christmas, baby showers, milestone birthdays. I'd gather everyone together, get them lined up, and try to make something worth holding onto.

What started as a way to hold onto my own family's memories slowly became something I couldn't put down — and eventually something I wanted to bring to other people's lives too.

Life Beyond San Diego

A decade in the Bay — and a trip to Japan that changed everything.

I spent over a decade living in the Bay Area, exploring San Francisco from end to end. The fog, the architecture, the neighborhoods that feel like different worlds — it showed me how differently light can fall depending on where you are, what time it is, what the city is doing around you.

San Francisco made me pay attention. San Diego made me love golden hour. And Japan — Japan made me slow down completely.

What I Love to Capture

Smiles, sunsets, milestones — and the quiet moments in between.

People

Celebrations

Birthdays, graduations, milestones, holidays — the moments you'll want to remember exactly as they happened.

Nature & Light

A San Diego sunset never gets old. I'm always chasing that last hour of light — the kind that makes everything glow.

Everyday Beauty

The beach at 7am. A quiet street. The details that make a place or a day feel like it matters.

My Approach

I want you to actually have fun.

Photo sessions can feel awkward — I know that. My whole job is making sure yours doesn't. I'll keep it moving, keep it light, and keep you laughing. The goal is a photo you'd genuinely want on your wall, in your phone, on your desk at work — something that looks like you on a really good day.

Let's Work Together

Ready to make something worth keeping?

Whether it's a portrait session, a family gathering, a birthday, or just a beautiful evening at the beach — I'd love to be there for it. Reach out and let's figure out what we can create.
