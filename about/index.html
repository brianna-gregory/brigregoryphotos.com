About Brianna Gregory

I’m a San Diego native with a love for capturing people, connection, and the small moments that become lasting memories.

Photography started as a hobby — and slowly became something more. I’ve always been drawn to creativity, emotion, and the way a single image can hold so much meaning.

Being born and raised in San Diego has shaped the way I see beauty. I love natural light, relaxed environments, and images that feel effortless but intentional.
