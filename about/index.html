The Beginning

It all started in 2018 with a camera.

I got my first professional camera in 2018 as a side hobby while living in Sonoma County. I was immediately obsessed. I'd spend weekends exploring nature—Bodega Bay, the Russian River, hidden trails everywhere. There's something about capturing the beauty of thunderstorms and raw natural light that just captivated me. I was studying for my bachelor's degree and MBA at the time, but photography stayed with me as my creative escape.

Evolution

Then I discovered the joy of capturing people and moments.

I started photographing my friends and family—at personal photoshoots, birthdays, holidays, baby showers, backyard hangouts, more professional events. There's something different about capturing people than landscapes. You're documenting relationships, joy, connection—the stuff that actually matters.

I moved back to my hometown in San Diego and thought I could take this further. Now I'm a product manager on the weekdays and a photographer on the weekends when inspiration strikes. I love laughing with multiple generations at events, bringing the fun and professionalism together. It's turned into something I truly enjoy.
