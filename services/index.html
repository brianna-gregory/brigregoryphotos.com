What’s Included

All services include professional editing and access to a digital gallery where you can download your final images. Each session is handled with care, attention to detail, and an editing style that keeps your photos clean, timeless, and true to the moment.

1. Submit your inquiry: Share the type of session or event you’re interested in, your preferred date, location, and any important details.

2. I’ll follow up shortly: After your inquiry is submitted online, I’ll review your request and get back to you. I may ask for more information to make sure your session is planned properly.

3. Deposit required: A deposit will be required to secure your booking date. Your date is not officially reserved until the deposit has been received.

4. Professional editing: Your selected images will be professionally edited to create a polished, cohesive final gallery.

5. Digital delivery: Once complete, your final images will be delivered through an online gallery with access to digitally download your images.

Services include, but are not limited to, graduation photos, maternity sessions, portraits, lifestyle sessions, and special events. If you have something specific in mind, submit an inquiry and I’ll be happy to review the details.
